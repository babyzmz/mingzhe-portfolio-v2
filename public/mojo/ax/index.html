Enterprise agent workbench · by OpenAX

Work for teams,not just one chat.

MojoAX is the enterprise sibling of MojoClaw: a workbench where the task in progress summons the surface it needs, with governance for teams. It is developed after MojoClaw and Mojo Core, so this page separates what exists in source from what is planned.

Why it exists.

Teams need a record.

Organisations need audit, approval and quotas around AI work, not just a faster chat.

Walls of panels slow people down.

Every capability on screen at once is noise. A surface should appear because the work needs it.

A renamed personal app is not an enterprise app.

Density, roles and deliverables differ. MojoAX keeps its own workflow on the same Core.

What it does.

Each item carries its real status. Nothing on this page is a runtime capture; placeholders say what will be shown.

01In development

Surfaces on demand

The agent's actual activity selects the surface — browser, terminal, code, files — with pin and split view for multi-monitor work. Present in source; not yet captured from a running build.

02In development

Tenants and single sign-on through Core

Sign-in sessions, tenant lists and tenant switching call Mojo Core. Present in source; external identity providers are not yet verified.

03In development

One Core, two products

MojoAX consumes the same Core adapter as MojoClaw: no gateway, model proxy or orchestration of its own.

04Planned

Governance: audit, approval, brand, quota

The enterprise layer that sets MojoAX apart. Defined as product intent; not built.

05Planned

Business-grade deliverables

Reports, SOPs and web tools produced and verified through Core's artifact pipeline.

Questions.

Why does MojoAX come after MojoClaw?+

Both run on Mojo Core. Getting Core and the personal workbench right first means the enterprise workbench starts from proven runs, permissions and previews.

Why are there no screenshots?+

Nothing has been captured from a running MojoAX build yet. The placeholders say what will be shown once it is.

What is OpenAX?+

The owner's product family name. MojoAX and MojoClaw are siblings under it, built on Mojo Core.

The same foundation, waiting for the team-sized version.
